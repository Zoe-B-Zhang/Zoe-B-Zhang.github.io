This project is a submission for the [Kaggle Nano-Banana Hackathon](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/banana/). Foresight Engine uses Google's Gemini 1.5 Flash to analyze a time-series of images and generate a photorealistic prediction of a future state.

## Project Overview

Foresight Engine is an AI-powered predictive vision tool that leverages advanced machine learning to forecast future visual states based on historical image sequences. The system analyzes patterns in time-series image data and generates realistic predictions of how a scene might evolve.

## Technology Stack

- **Core AI Engine:** Google Gemini 1.5 Flash
- **Development Environment:** Google Colab
- **Core Libraries:** `google-generativeai`, `Pillow`, `Matplotlib`, `pathlib`
